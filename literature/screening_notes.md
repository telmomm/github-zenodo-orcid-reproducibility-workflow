## Included Studies

Studies were included when they discussed at least one of:

- computational reproducibility
- version control
- software citation
- persistent identifiers
- research software
- open science infrastructure

## Coding Note

Bibliographic fields in `included_studies.csv` are taken verbatim from the
reference manager export (`../state of the art/SOTA.ris`). The analytical fields
(discipline, reproducibility problem, barrier, proposed solution, platform flags)
and the twelve binary requirement dimensions in `included_studies.csv` and
`extraction_template.csv` were coded from titles, abstracts and the state-of-the-art
synthesis. They are the working coding and are subject to author validation against
the full texts.
