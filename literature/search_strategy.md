# Literature Search Strategy

## Objective

To identify publications addressing:

- reproducible research
- computational reproducibility
- research software
- open science
- software citation
- GitHub
- Zenodo
- ORCID

---

## Databases

- Scopus
- Web of Science
- Google Scholar (supplementary)

---

## Search Date

2026-08-20

---

## Search Queries

### Core reproducibility search

("reproducible research" OR
"research reproducibility" OR
"computational reproducibility")
AND
("open science" OR
"research transparency" OR
"research software")

### GitHub search

GitHub AND reproducibility AND research

### Zenodo search

Zenodo AND reproducibility

### ORCID search

ORCID AND research reproducibility

### Software citation search

"software citation" AND reproducibility

---

## Inclusion Criteria

- Peer-reviewed publications.
- English language.
- Published between 2015 and 2026.
- Relevant to computational reproducibility.

---

## Exclusion Criteria

- Duplicate publications.
- Non-scientific publications.
- Studies unrelated to reproducibility.
